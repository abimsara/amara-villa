The Villa

Designed for slow living

A private three-suite villa where contemporary design meets the tropical south of Sri Lanka.

Our Story

Built around the garden

Serendib House was designed by a Colombo architect and built by local craftsmen, using white plaster walls, teak joinery and reclaimed terracotta throughout. The layout is deliberately simple: three suites arranged around a central courtyard and pool, so every room catches the sea breeze.

The villa sleeps up to eight guests and can be booked room-by-room or exclusively for the whole property — a favourite for family gatherings and small celebrations.

A Typical Day

Nothing on the schedule

Morning. Coffee on the veranda, breakfast served whenever you wake, and a swim before the heat settles in.

Afternoon. A short tuk-tuk ride to Ahangama town for lunch, or stay put with a book by the pool and fresh king coconut.

Evening. Sunset on the beach, then dinner prepared by the villa chef — Sri Lankan curries or something simpler, your call.
