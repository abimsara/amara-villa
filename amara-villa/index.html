Welcome to Serendib House

Slow mornings, salt air
and nowhere to be

Serendib House is a private three-suite retreat set among coconut palms, fifteen minutes from Galle Fort. Designed for families, friends and couples who want the comfort of a boutique hotel with the privacy of their own home.

Wake up to the ocean, swim in the infinity pool, and let our resident team take care of breakfast, transfers and everything in between.
